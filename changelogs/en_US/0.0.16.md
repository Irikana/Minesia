# 0.0.16

## 2026.5.8

***

### New Features

- Added Vibrant Visuals support, implementing a warm-toned, high-contrast visual style through color grading configuration, with subtle metallic texture on custom items

### Improvements

- Significantly reduced Victor's (Equipment Trader) gold coin trade prices, all gold coin purchases are at least halved:
  - Blaze Sword: 20 gold coins → 8 gold coins
  - Ender Pearl Sword: 25 gold coins → 10 gold coins
  - Forest Sword: 20 gold coins → 8 gold coins
  - Winter Sword: 10 gold coins → 4 gold coins
  - Pioneer: 10 gold coins → 4 gold coins
  - Selfish Sword: 10 gold coins → 4 gold coins
  - Statue Totem: 15 gold coins → 6 gold coins
  - Desert Scythe: 10 gold coins → 4 gold coins
  - Life Stone: 5 diamond coins → 2 diamond coins
  - Black Dagger: 15 stone coins → 8 stone coins
  - White Golden Sword: 25 stone coins → 12 stone coins

- Adjusted copper equipment stats to be better than stone but weaker than iron:
  - Copper Dagger damage: 2~4 → 3~5
  - Copper Scythe damage: 5.5~6.5 → 6~7.5
  - Copper Dagger durability: 64 → 150
  - Copper Scythe durability: 130 → 280
